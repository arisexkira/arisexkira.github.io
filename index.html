What is KIRA?

KIRA is a personal voice assistant for Windows PCs, written in Python. It listens continuously, understands plain English and carries out your commands.

  • Open and close apps, websites and files
  • Volume, mute, brightness and media keys
  • Screenshots, lock the PC, weather, time and date
  • Reminders and notes
